## Abstract

This paper proposes a hierarchical reinforcement learning framework for cooperative caching and SWIPT-enabled cognitive IoT systems. The proposed approach jointly optimizes spectrum access, caching decisions, and energy management in dynamic wireless environments using intelligent learning-based policies.
