Welcome! I am a researcher working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and future wireless communication systems.

My research focuses on AI-native wireless networks, reinforcement learning, multi-agent learning, UAV-assisted communications, cache-enabled networks, and intelligent resource allocation for next-generation communication systems.

I completed my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Concordia University, where my thesis focused on artificial intelligence for cognitive, adaptive, and autonomous future wireless networks. I am currently continuing my research at ÉTS in Montreal, working on AI-driven optimization and intelligent decision-making for next-generation wireless systems.

My broader goal is to bridge AI and wireless engineering by developing learning-based methods that can make future communication networks more adaptive, autonomous, and efficient.
