Welcome! I am a postdoctoral researcher working on AI-native future wireless networks, intelligent communication systems, and reinforcement learning for next-generation wireless environments.

My research focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence and wireless communications, including:

- Multi-agent reinforcement learning
- UAV-assisted wireless networks
- Cache-enabled communication systems
- Intelligent resource allocation
- Wireless network optimization
- Realistic channel-aware simulation frameworks

I completed my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Concordia University, where my research focused on AI-driven optimization techniques for future wireless systems.

I am currently continuing my research at ÉTS (École de technologie supérieure) in Montreal, exploring scalable AI methods for intelligent wireless networks and next-generation communication architectures.

I am particularly interested in bridging the gap between AI systems and wireless engineering to help shape the future of AI-native communication networks.

Feel free to connect with me for research collaborations, academic discussions, or industry opportunities related to AI and wireless communications.
