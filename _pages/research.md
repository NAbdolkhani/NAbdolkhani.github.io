# Research

My research focuses on AI-driven and AI-native wireless communication systems, with an emphasis on reinforcement learning, multi-agent learning, and intelligent network optimization.

## Research Interests

- AI-native future wireless networks
- Reinforcement learning for wireless systems
- Multi-agent learning and CTDE frameworks
- UAV-assisted wireless networks
- Device-to-device communications
- Cache-enabled communication systems
- Intelligent resource allocation
- Cognitive and autonomous wireless networks
- Sionna RT-based realistic wireless simulation

## Current Direction

I am currently interested in scalable AI methods for next-generation wireless networks, especially methods that combine realistic wireless environments with multi-agent learning, resource allocation, and intelligent network control.
